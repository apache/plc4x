= Eclipse Ditto



https://eclipse.dev/ditto/[Eclipse Ditto] - "… where IoT devices and their digital twins get together" - manages the
digital twins of IoT devices.

PLC4X can be used to feed such a twin with live data from a PLC.

This integration is maintained by the Eclipse Ditto project.
A complete example is available in their examples repository:
https://github.com/eclipse-ditto/ditto-examples/tree/master/plc4x[ditto-examples / plc4x].
