= TLS-PSK

TLS authenticated with a *pre-shared key* rather than certificates. Both sides are configured with
the same identity and key, and no certificate infrastructure is involved - which is what makes it
usable on devices too small to validate a certificate chain, and on plants with no PKI.

It ships in the same artifact as the xref:transports/tls.adoc[TLS transport] and is selected with
its own transport code.

== Transport syntax

----
{IP address or hostname}[:{port}]
----

.Example
[source]
----
modbus-tcp:tls-psk://192.168.1.100:802?tls-psk.psk-identity=line-3&tls-psk.psk-key=00112233445566778899aabbccddeeff
----

include::partial$transport-tls-psk.adoc[]

`tls-psk.psk-identity` and `tls-psk.psk-key` belong together - the key is hex-encoded, and the
connection fails if only one of the two is given. The remaining socket options are inherited from
the xref:transports/tcp.adoc[TCP transport].

TIP: `tls-psk.log-session-keys=true` writes the session keys into the
xref:audit-log.adoc[audit-log] in `SSLKEYLOGFILE` format, so a Wireshark capture of the connection
can be decrypted. That hands anyone holding the log the ability to read the traffic - use it on a
test system.
